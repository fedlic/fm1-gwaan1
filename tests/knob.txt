# GWAAN-1 v0.2.1 knob response: SHORT windows - 3 s immediately before
# vs 3 s immediately after a knob jump. (v0.2 was verified on 40 s
# averages, which hid exactly what the user heard on the device: turn
# the knob, nothing happens.) Analysed offline by analyze_v021.py.
# Part A (KNOB2 DEPTH): settle at 0, capture, jump to 127, capture.
# The 150-600 Hz body band must jump within the first seconds.
wait 600
turn K4 -10
wait 100
expect k4 0
turn K2 -16
wait 100
expect k2 0
master 4096
wait 3000
wav k2a.wav
wait 3000
wavstop
turn K2 32
wait 100
expect k2 127
wav k2b.wav
wait 3000
wavstop
